Product Requirements Document
1. Customer Order Detail Page
1.1 Order Tracking Stepper — Delivered Status
When an order reaches the “Delivered” status, the tracking stepper does not visually show all previous stages as completed. The expected behavior is that once an order is delivered, the full progress path should appear completed, with all prior steps clearly marked as finished.

1.2 Missing Line Item Information in Customer Order Details
On the customer order detail page, some order items display incorrect pricing, including showing a price of zero, and variant-specific information is not visible. This issue is especially noticeable for products with multiple variants. Customers should be able to see the correct item price along with the selected variant attributes for each purchased item.

2. Product Detail Page Attribute Display — Color Options
2.1 Color Attributes Need Visual Swatches
Color-based product attributes are currently shown only as text, which makes the selection experience unclear. Color options should display a visible color swatch alongside the color name so users can identify the option visually. The current checkbox-style presentation is not appropriate for color selection and should be replaced with a cleaner visual treatment that highlights the actual color.

3. Product Detail Page Sticky CTA and Variant/Cart Sync
3.1 Quantity Display Does Not Update When Switching Variants
When a customer adds a quantity for one variant of a product and then switches to another variant, the sticky call-to-action still shows the quantity from the previously selected variant. The quantity shown should always reflect the currently selected variant only.

3.2 Product Page Does Not Reflect Existing Cart State on Return Visit
If a customer adds an item to the cart, leaves the product page, and later returns, the product page may incorrectly show the default add-to-cart state instead of the actual quantity already in the cart. The page should immediately reflect the current cart state when loaded again.

3.3 Unit Selection Is Not Preserved
For products that use unit-based pricing or selection, the chosen unit is not remembered when the customer navigates away and comes back. Previously selected units should remain consistent so the customer does not need to reselect them.

3.4 Incorrect CTA Behavior for Unit-Based Products
For products that are purchased by unit selection rather than by simple quantity incrementing, the current CTA behavior is incorrect. In these cases, the experience should guide the customer through unit-based purchase flow and then move them toward cart review, rather than showing the standard quantity counter behavior.

4. Product Card CTA Behavior
4.1 Direct Add-to-Cart for Simple Products
Products that do not require attribute selection or unit selection currently lack the most direct purchase action on the product card. These simple products should allow customers to add them to the cart immediately from the listing interface without unnecessary extra steps.

4.2 Price Inquiry Call-to-Action
Some products require a price inquiry flow instead of a standard purchase flow. In these cases, the product card should clearly present a contact or inquiry action instead of a normal purchase CTA. This should support direct customer contact through the configured support channel and behave appropriately across devices.

5. Reorder Experience for Returning Customers
Returning customers currently do not have a dedicated way to quickly browse products they have ordered before. A reorder experience is needed so customers can easily view previously purchased products and add them again with minimal friction.

6. Discount Entry on Product Management
The product creation/editing experience currently handles discount input in a way that is unclear or inconsistent. Discounting should be based on a percentage model, with clear visibility into how the final price is derived from the original price and the entered discount value.

Summary of Required Outcomes
Delivered orders must show complete progress in the tracking stepper.
Customer order details must show correct prices and selected variant information.
Color attributes must be visually represented with swatches.
Product detail page CTA state must always stay in sync with the selected variant and the cart.
Unit-based selections must be preserved and handled with the correct purchase flow.
Simple products should support direct add-to-cart from product cards.
Products requiring manual pricing should show a clear inquiry/contact action.
Returning customers should be able to quickly reorder previously purchased products.
Discount handling in product management should be percentage-based and transparent.
